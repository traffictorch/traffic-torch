Announcing OFUX – Our Brand New 3‑in‑1 Audit Dashboard

2026-08-30

Zero FUX Given – The OFUX Dashboard Is Live

We’re excited to unveil the biggest upgrade to the Traffic Torch homepage since we launched: the OFUX Dashboard.

OFUX stands for Online Findability & User Experience. It combines three independent audits – UX Health, SEO Intent, and AI Search – into a single, clean dashboard. And yes, we’ve given it the tagline “Zero FUX Given” because we’ve stripped away all the fluff you usually find in SEO tools.

What’s new?

Previously, we had a basic SEO tool that did a decent job but only scratched the surface. We knew we could do better. So we rebuilt everything from the ground up. Now, when you enter a URL, you get:

  • A UX Health score that checks readability, navigation, accessibility, mobile readiness, and performance proxies.
  • An SEO Intent score that measures E‑E‑A‑T signals, content depth, schema markup, and intent alignment.
  • An AI Search (AEO) score that evaluates answerability, structured data, scannability, conversational tone, originality, and anti‑AI safety.

All three scores are shown side‑by‑side, so you can see at a glance where your page stands. If you need more detail, each card has a “Full Results” button that takes you to the standalone tool for a deep dive.

AI‑generated CMS fixer (beta)

We also added an AI‑generated CMS fixer (beta). After the audit, you can click “Generate AI Fixes” and we’ll detect your CMS (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, etc.) and give you three specific, actionable recommendations – without inventing fake menu paths.

We ran a self‑audit on our own site and saw our overall score improve from around 70 to over 80 after applying the fixes. We’re confident this tool will help you find and fix issues that are holding your site back.

Privacy‑first, always

The best part? Everything runs in your browser. We don’t store or log any data – it’s completely privacy‑first.

Try it out at https://traffictorch.net/ – we’d love to hear your feedback. And as always, zero fux given.
