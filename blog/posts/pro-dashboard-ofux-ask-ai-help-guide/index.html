1. Dashboard Overview

The Traffic Torch dashboard is your central command centre. It brings together all your tools, analytics, audit history, and settings in one place. You can access it at https://traffictorch.net/dashboard/ after logging in (or as a guest with limited features).

Layout Overview

  • Sidebar (desktop only): Collapsible navigation with your logo and main menu.
  • Horizontal Tabs: Dashboard, Audits, Analytics, Real‑time, GSC, Integrations, Upgrade, Settings.
  • Quick Audit Form: Instantly run any of the 15 tools on any URL.
  • Epic Cards: At-a-glance metrics from GA4 and GSC (sessions, users, clicks, impressions, etc.).
  • Tools Grid: Quick links to all individual tools.
  • Recent Audits: Your last 5–10 audits with copy, re‑scan, and delete options.

The dashboard adapts to your plan – Free users see demo data and a 5‑audit history; Pro and Enterprise users get full data sync and higher limits.

2. OFUX – The All‑in‑One Audit

OFUX stands for Online Findability & User Experience. It's our flagship tool that analyses any page across three critical pillars in one view:

  • UX Health – Readability, navigation, accessibility, mobile/PWA, and performance proxies.
  • SEO Intent – E‑E‑A‑T signals, content depth, schema markup, and on‑page SEO.
  • AI Search (AEO) – Answerability, structured data, scannability, conversational tone, uniqueness, and anti‑AI safety.

How to Run an OFUX Audit

  • From the Homepage: Enter a URL in the main input field and click “Analyze OFUX”.
  • From the Dashboard: Use the Quick Audit form – select any tool (each focuses on one pillar). For the full three‑pillar view, stick to the homepage.

Understanding Your Results

After analysis, you'll see three cards – one per pillar. Each card shows:

  • A 0–100 score with a coloured ring (green = excellent, orange = good, red = needs work).
  • A grade emoji and textual rating.
  • A detailed breakdown of individual metrics, each marked ✅ (pass), ⚠️ (average), or ❌ (fail).
  • A “Full Results →” button that opens the dedicated tool page for deeper analysis and AI fixes.

Below the cards, you'll find an expandable “See the Full Checklist” – a quick reference of what the audit looks for (short paragraphs, author bylines, etc.) and common pitfalls (missing alt text, passive voice).

3. Ask Traffic Torch AI

The Ask Traffic Torch AI feature is your personal assistant, available on every tool page and the OFUX dashboard. It uses advanced LLMs to interpret your audit results and give you honest, actionable advice.

Where to Find It

  • On the Homepage: After running an OFUX audit, you'll see a text area labelled “Ask Traffic Torch AI”.
  • On any Tool Page: Scroll down to the AI Assistant section.
  • On the Dashboard: Use the “Ask AI” button next to any saved audit.

How to Use It

  1. Type a question (e.g., “Why did my UX Health score drop?” or “How can I improve my AI Search score?”).
  2. Click the button or press Enter.
  3. The AI responds within seconds with context‑aware, step‑by‑step guidance.
  4. Ask follow‑up questions to dive deeper.

What the AI Knows

  • Your latest audit results – all scores, metric statuses, and failed items.
  • If you've connected GA4 or GSC, it also has access to your real analytics data (sessions, clicks, bounce rates, etc.) to correlate with audit findings.
  • It does not have access to your personal data or browser history – only the current audit context.

Note: Each AI query counts toward your daily audit limit (same as running a tool). Free users get 3 per day, Pro 25, Enterprise 300.

4. Connecting GA4 & GSC

To see your real analytics data instead of demos, connect your Google Analytics 4 and Search Console accounts via the Integrations tab.

Connect GA4

  1. Go to the Integrations tab.
  2. In the GA4 card, click “Connect”.
  3. Authorise Traffic Torch via Google – you'll be redirected to grant permissions.
  4. After redirect, enter your GA4 Property ID (found in your GA4 admin settings).
  5. Your dashboard will now display real metrics on the Epic Cards and Analytics tab.

Connect GSC

  1. In the Integrations tab, click “Connect” on the GSC card.
  2. Authorise via Google.
  3. Enter your GSC site URL – you can use full URL (e.g., https://example.com/) or domain‑property format (sc-domain:example.com). The tool auto‑converts.
  4. Your GSC data will appear on the Epic Cards, the GSC tab, and will be used by the AI.

Managing Connections

  • Use the “Disconnect” button in the Integrations tab to remove any connection.
  • Use “Refresh” buttons on cards or tabs to manually fetch the latest data.

5. Analytics Tab (GA4)

The Analytics tab is your full GA4 dashboard. It includes:

  • Key Metrics – Sessions, Users, New Users, Page Views, Conversion Rate (with percentage changes).
  • Overview Chart – Line chart showing Sessions and Users over time.
  • New vs Returning Users – Doughnut chart.
  • Traffic Sources – Pie chart (sessions) and bar chart (key events).
  • Device & Geographic Breakdowns – Doughnut and bar charts.
  • Top Content & Top Converting Pages – Lists with pagination and CSV export (Pro+).

Date Range Controls

  • Choose 1, 7, 14, 30 days, or Custom (Enterprise only).
  • Changing the range auto‑refreshes the data.

Export CSV

Pro and Enterprise users can export Top Content and Top Converting Pages as CSV files using the Export CSV button.

The Real‑time tab is available exclusively for Enterprise users. It gives you a live view of user activity on your site, updating automatically every 15 seconds.

  • Active Users by Minute – line chart showing the trend.

7. GSC Tab (Search Console)

The GSC tab mirrors the Analytics tab but focuses on Search Console data. You'll see:

  • Clicks, Impressions, CTR, Average Position – with percentage changes.
  • Trend Chart – Clicks and Impressions over time.

Pro and Enterprise users can export Top Queries and Top Pages as CSV files.

8. Audit History & Management

All your audits are automatically saved in the Audits tab. Here's what you can do:

  • View – table shows date, tool, and URL.
  • Copy – copy the URL to your clipboard.
  • Scan – re‑run the same audit on the same URL.
  • Delete – permanently remove the audit.
  • Load More – Free users see 5, Pro 10, Enterprise all history.

Guest vs. Logged‑in: Guests store audits locally (expire after 24h, max 5). Logged‑in users sync to the cloud – no expiration.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account to use Traffic Torch? ▼

No – the homepage OFUX tool and all individual tools work without an account. However, creating a free account unlocks the full dashboard, audit history, and ability to connect GA4/GSC.

Is my data safe when using Traffic Torch? ▼

Yes. All audits run client‑side – your page content is never sent to our servers. Your GA4/GSC connections are secured via OAuth, and we only store the minimal data needed for your dashboard (like metrics summaries). We never sell or share your data.

What if I hit my daily audit limit? ▼

You can upgrade to a higher plan to get more daily audits. Alternatively, you can wait until the next day (midnight UTC) for your limit to reset.

Can I use the AI assistant without running an audit? ▼

The AI works best when it has audit results to reference. On the homepage, you must run an OFUX audit first. On tool pages, the AI is automatically aware of that tool's results.

Why do I see demo data in the analytics cards? ▼

Demo data appears when you're not logged in or haven't connected your own GA4/GSC accounts. Connect your accounts to replace it with your real metrics.
