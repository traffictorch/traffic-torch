How Search Engines Evaluate Your Page

Modern search engines go far beyond keywords. They analyze hundreds of signals to determine if your content truly satisfies what users are searching for.

  • Does the page render correctly in headless browsers used by AI crawlers?
  • Is the DOM stable during load, or does content appear late and get missed?
  • Can AI extract clean text easily from your HTML structure?
  • Is your structured data parsed reliably without errors?
  • Can AI crawlers access your content without JS barriers or cookie walls?
  • Is your text-to-code ratio high enough for AI engines to prefer your page?
  • Is your heading hierarchy, paragraph segmentation, and list clarity predictable?
  • Are render-blocking scripts delaying DOM availability for extractors?
  • Is your content stable after initial load, or does it disappear before capture?

This AEO performance analyzer replicates that evaluation process and gives you an objective score out of 100, plus prioritized, actionable fixes.

On-Page AEO Performance Checklist

Master these proven on-page AEO best practices to boost search visibility, AI citation, user experience and rankings.

AEO Performance Checklist 🗝️

✅ Best Practices to Do

  • ✅ Ensure headless render matches: AI crawlers use headless browsers; hydration mismatches and JS errors break extraction.
  • ✅ Keep DOM stable: Avoid late-inserted nodes and script-driven rewrites that appear after initial capture.
  • ✅ Use semantic HTML: Clean heading hierarchy, low div-soup density, predictable paragraph segmentation.
  • ✅ Validate JSON-LD: No parse errors, no warnings, reasonable nesting depth.
  • ✅ Allow crawler access: Friendly robots.txt, no JS-gated content, no infinite scroll traps, no cookie walls.
  • ✅ Maximize text density: High text-to-code, text-to-ads, text-to-scripts ratios.
  • ✅ Maintain semantic structure: Correct headings, clear lists, readable tables, consistent entity naming.
  • ✅ Minimize render blocking: JS that blocks DOM, CSS that delays text, third-party scripts delaying content.
  • ✅ Stabilize content: No late injection, no dynamic replacement, no hydration flicker, no SPA route changes before capture.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • ❌ Ignoring headless render: Assuming visual rendering equals headless extraction.
  • ❌ Late content injection: Content that appears after AI crawler captures DOM.
  • ❌ Div-soup HTML: No semantic structure, making extraction hard.
  • ❌ Broken JSON-LD: Parse errors and warnings that block schema understanding.
  • ❌ JS-gated content: Content behind JS that AI crawlers cannot execute.
  • ❌ Infinite scroll traps: No pagination fallback for crawlers.
  • ❌ Cookie walls: Blocking AI crawlers from seeing content.
  • ❌ Render-blocking scripts: Delaying DOM availability for extractors.
  • ❌ Unstable SPAs: Route changes and hydration flicker that lose content.

AEO Performance FAQ

Traffic Torch instantly scans your site's technical performance for AEO and GEO. Then delivers fixes and education to align with modern extraction standards.

Why is render fidelity so important for AEO?▼
AI crawlers use headless browsers. If your page doesn’t render correctly in headless mode — missing content, hydration mismatches, JS errors — extraction fails. This means your content never reaches the ranking or citation stage. Traffic Torch detects these issues in seconds and suggests targeted fixes.
How does DOM stability affect AI extraction?▼
AI scrapers capture the DOM once. If your content appears later via late-inserted nodes or script-driven rewrites, it’s invisible to the extractor. Stable DOM means content is present at initial capture. Traffic Torch measures mutation rate and flags unstable patterns.
What is content extractability and why does it matter?▼
Extractability is how easy it is for AI to pull clean text from your HTML. Semantic HTML, clean heading hierarchy, low div-soup density, and predictable paragraph segmentation make extraction reliable. Traffic Torch audits these signals and provides fixes.
How can I improve schema parse performance?▼
AI engines use schema as a primary signal for entity understanding. Ensure your JSON-LD parses without errors, has no warnings, reasonable nesting depth, and consistent cross-page coherence. Traffic Torch validates your schema and gives exact fixes.
What makes a page crawler-accessible for AI?▼
AI crawlers are more fragile than Googlebot. They need friendly robots.txt, no JS-gated content, no infinite scroll traps, no cookie-wall blocking, and no dynamic route hiding. Traffic Torch checks all of these and suggests fixes.
Why does text density matter for AI engines?▼
AI engines prefer high-density, low-noise pages. A high text-to-code ratio, text-to-ads ratio, and text-to-scripts ratio means more extractable content per KB. Traffic Torch measures these ratios and flags pages with low density.
