Frequently Asked Questions

Why build NUSA instead of a traditional SEO tool? ↓
Standalone SEO tools ignore UX, and standalone UX tools ignore AI search. After auditing hundreds of sites we saw that the real winners master all three. NUSA gives you a single dashboard that tells you where you actually stand across UX Health, SEO Intent and AI Search (AEO) — in one pass, in your browser.
How does on-page SEO affect findability in 2026? ↓
In our testing, pages that nail title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure and internal links see a 20–40% increase in qualified organic traffic. But you also need to satisfy user intent and be ready for AI overviews. Sites with perfect technical SEO can still lose traffic when their content doesn't answer the user's actual question. NUSA checks both on-page signals and semantic relevance.
What's the biggest UX mistake we see? ↓
Two things: ignoring mobile tap targets under 44px, and using long dense paragraphs. In one case study we boosted a client's dwell time by 60% just by breaking up paragraphs and increasing font size to 18px. NUSA flags these exact issues so you can fix them in minutes.
Does accessibility really help SEO? ↓
Yes. Accessible sites tend to rank better because they offer a better user experience, and WCAG compliance often improves crawl efficiency — search engines can understand your page structure more easily. NUSA checks alt text, contrast, semantic HTML and landmarks so you can hit the WCAG 2.2 AA standard without hiring an expert.
How does content quality affect AI citations? ↓
AI answer engines like Perplexity and ChatGPT rely on clear, structured, authoritative content. Adding a concise definition in the first 150 words, using FAQ schema and including first-hand data measurably boosts citation rate. NUSA's AI Search module measures exactly these signals — answerability, structured data, unique insights — to tell you how likely you are to be quoted.
How can I quickly improve my NUSA score? ↓
Three-step sprint: (1) Add an author byline and bio on every post — often a 15-point jump in Expertise just from that. (2) Break long paragraphs into 2–3 sentence chunks — readability scores rise 10–20 points. (3) Create a Contact page and link to it in the footer — trustworthiness improves immediately. Then run the audit again and watch the numbers climb.

Connect your GA4 and GSC properties once. The dashboard then pulls in:

Pro and Enterprise store history in the cloud. It syncs across devices. Free users get local storage with a 5‑audit limit.
