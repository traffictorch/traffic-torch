How OFUX Works? (Zero FUX) ↓

The OFUX Dashboard – Instant Health Check

OFUX stands for Online Findability & User Experience. It’s a single‑page dashboard that runs three independent audits in parallel: UX Health, SEO Intent, and AI Search (AEO). Each tool uses its own proven scoring logic – the same as our standalone tools – but displayed together for a complete 360° view of your site’s performance.

Everything runs inside your browser. We fetch the page via a secure proxy, parse the DOM, and compute all scores on‑device. No data is ever stored or logged. Your privacy is guaranteed.

The Three Pillars of OFUX

🧠

UX Health

Readability, navigation, accessibility, mobile/PWA readiness, and performance proxies. Shows where users get stuck or leave.

🎯

SEO Intent

E‑E‑A‑T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), content depth, readability, and schema markup – aligns your content with search intent.

🤖

AI Search (AEO)

Answerability, structured data, EEAT, scannability, conversational tone, originality, and anti‑AI safety – optimised for AI‑powered search engines (Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT).

AI‑Generated Fixes (Beta)

  • After the audit, click “Generate AI Fixes” – we’ll detect your CMS and give you 3 specific, actionable recommendations.
  • The AI is trained to give honest advice – no fake menu paths. If it’s unsure, it tells you.
  • We also provide a “Full Results” button for each module, so you can dive deeper into the standalone tool if you need more detail.

* The AI fixer is a beta feature – your feedback helps us improve it.

Zero FUX Given

We built OFUX to cut through the noise. No fluff, no dark patterns – just honest, actionable data about your site’s findability and user experience. Use it to fix what’s broken, and let your users (and search engines) do the rest.

We wanted something different. A tool that actually measures what matters – your page’s findability (will people find it?) and user experience (will they stay and convert?).

So we built OFUX – Online Findability & User Experience. No fluff. No dark patterns. Just three real audits:

OFUX Checklist – What We Actually Check

We don’t just give you a score. We tell you exactly what to fix, where to look, and how to improve – for UX, SEO, and AI.

✅ What We Look For

  • ✔ Readable sentences (under 20 words) and short paragraphs
  • ✔ Clear headings and scannable structure
  • ✔ Author bylines, bios, and citations
  • ✔ Mobile‑friendly design and touch targets
  • ✔ Fast loading and optimised assets
  • ✔ Structured data (FAQ, Article, Person)
  • ✔ First‑person language and engaging questions
  • ✔ Fresh, original insights and case studies

❌ Common Pitfalls

  • ✘ Walls of text with no breaks
  • ✘ Missing alt text or low contrast
  • ✘ No about, contact, or policy pages
  • ✘ Slow Core Web Vitals
  • ✘ No schema or outdated schema
  • ✘ Passive voice and vague language
  • ✘ Repetitive, generic AI‑sounding content
  • ✘ No clear answer in the first 150 words
