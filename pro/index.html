Frequently Asked Questions

How many daily audits do I get with each plan?

Free: 3 audits per day. Pro: 25 audits per day. Enterprise: 300 audits per day. Limits reset at midnight UTC.

What happens when I hit my daily limit?

You'll see a friendly notification that you've reached your daily run limit. Your account automatically resets at midnight UTC, and you can continue auditing the next day.

How does billing work for Pro and Enterprise?

Pro is $6 per month or $48 per year (billed annually). Enterprise is $68 per month or $680 per year. Payments are processed securely via Stripe. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard.

Is there a refund policy?

Yes – full refund within 7 days of purchase if you're not satisfied. Contact support via the feedback form or contact page (link in footer). After 7 days, no prorated refunds as it's an annual plan.

Do audits run on your servers or mine?

All analysis is 100% client‑side in your browser using a secure CORS proxy to fetch HTML. No data is stored, logged, or sent to our servers – maximum privacy.

Can I use my subscription on multiple devices?

Yes – log in with your account on any device or browser. Your subscription is tied to your login, not to a specific device.

How do I upgrade or change my plan?

Visit the dedicated upgrade page at /upgrade/ to choose your plan and complete payment. If you're already a Pro or Enterprise user, you can manage your subscription from your dashboard.

Will new tools and features be added for Pro users?

Yes – Pro and Enterprise subscribers receive priority updates and early access to new modules (e.g., expanded AI tools, forecasting) at no extra cost.
