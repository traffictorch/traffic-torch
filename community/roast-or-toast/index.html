💡 How does it work? ▶

1. Paste an idea, a one-liner, or a full URL — anything from "an AI tool for plumbers" to "https://example.com".

2. Pick a mode. 🔥 Roast delivers a cutting, Simon Cowell-style critique — funny but always constructive, ending with 3 specific fixes. 🍞 Toast is a hype-man response — spotlights 3 concrete strengths and one actionable next-level nudge.

3. AI thinks using Cloudflare Workers AI - usually 3–6 seconds. For URLs it also fetches the page's Open Graph metadata (title, description, image) to inform the response.

4. Publish (optional). Tick "Make public" to turn the assessment into a torch on the Community Feed — others can comment, and Google indexes the generated page via DiscussionForumPosting schema. Perfect for long-tail SEO.

FAQ

What's the difference between a Roast and a Toast?

Roast = brutally honest critique (Simon Cowell energy — cutting but constructive). Toast = hype-man praise with one next-level nudge.

Can I publish my result?

Yes — tick "Make public" and the AI's assessment becomes a torch on the Community Feed that others can comment on.
