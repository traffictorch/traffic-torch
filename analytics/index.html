Traffic Torch, In the Open

Real GA4 and Google Search Console aggregates — the same numbers the site owner sees in the dashboard. Public because we think analytics shouldn't be private.

What's public: sessions, users, pageviews, engagement rate, clicks, impressions, CTR, average search position, top pages, and channel mix (Organic Search / Direct / Referral / Organic Social).

What's never public: personal data, search query text, referrer URLs, geographic breakdown, or device split. Refresh: every 6 hours. Date range: ends 2 days ago (Google's own lag).

Why make this public?

Because hiding analytics is a choice, not a requirement. Sharing real numbers keeps us honest, shows what's working, and lets anyone sanity-check our claims.
