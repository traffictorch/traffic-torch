Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Traffic Torch leaderboard? ↓
A public, all-time ranking of the highest-scoring websites audited by any Traffic Torch tool — NUSA, Keyword, Lighthouse Plus, AEO Performance, AI Search, Voice Search and more. Every entry is opt-in: a site only appears after its owner clicks Submit to Leaderboard on a completed audit.
How are leaderboard scores calculated? ↓
Each Traffic Torch tool produces a 0–100 score. The leaderboard displays that same score exactly as the tool reported it — no averaging, no editorial tweaks. When a URL is re-audited, the higher of the two scores is kept, so a site's best result always stands.
How do I get my site on the leaderboard? ↓
Run any Traffic Torch audit on your page, wait for the results, then click Submit to Leaderboard below the share panel. One entry per URL per tool. No account required to appear.
What data is stored on the leaderboard? ↓
Only the audited URL, page title, overall score and per-module score breakdown. No personal data, no IP addresses, no analytics. Audits you run but don't submit never leave your browser.
Can I remove my site from the leaderboard? ↓
Yes. Any entry can be hidden on request. Use the Contact link in the footer and include the exact URL that was submitted.
