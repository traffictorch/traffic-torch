What the Extension Actually Does

Title, meta, headings, internal links, keyword coverage, and — crucially — whether the page matches the intent a user actually typed.

🧠

UX Health

Readability, tap-target size, contrast, landmark structure, mobile readiness, and friction points that make users bounce.

🤖

AI Search (AEO)

Answerability, structured data, first-150-word clarity, and how likely Perplexity, Gemini and ChatGPT are to quote your page.

How the Browser Extension Works ↓

1. Install from the Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons or Microsoft Edge Add-ons.

2. Navigate to any page you want to audit — your own site, a competitor's landing page, or a client's product page.

3. Click the Traffic Torch icon. The extension reads the rendered DOM (what users and AI crawlers see) and returns a 0–100 score plus per-module breakdown.

4. Fix what matters. Every flagged item comes with a plain-English explanation and an actionable next step. No vanity metrics.

5. Optional: sign in to save audit history, sync across devices, and lift your daily limit.

Everything up to step 4 runs client-side. Your page content never leaves your machine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the Traffic Torch browser extensions free?
Yes. All three extensions – Chrome, Firefox and Edge – are 100% free. You get 3 audits per day on the Free tier, and the same login unlocks Pro (25/day) or Enterprise (300/day) limits across every integration.
Which browsers does Traffic Torch support?
Official extensions for Google Chrome (also works in Chromium, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera), Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge.
Does the extension send my data to a server?
No. The core audits – SEO Intent, UX Health, accessibility, AI-search signals – run client-side. Your page content never leaves your device. Only optional account features (saved history, cloud reports) touch the server, and only after you log in.
What can I audit with the browser extension?
Any page you can open in a tab: your own site during development, a competitor's landing page, a client's WordPress site, a Shopify product page, or a Framer landing page. It reads the rendered DOM — what users and AI crawlers actually see.
Do I need an account to use the extension?
No. Guest audits work out of the box. Signing in unlocks 25 audits/day (Pro) or 300/day (Enterprise), saved history and cross-device sync — the same account works across all Traffic Torch tools.
Is the browser extension the same as the Developer API?
Same audit engine, different workflow. The extension is for human-in-the-loop audits while browsing. The Developer API is for programmatic access inside your own apps, CI pipelines and automation. You can use both with the same account.
Will the extension slow down my browser?
No. It activates only when you click it, uses Manifest V3, and runs no continuous background scripts. Zero measurable overhead.
